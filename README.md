# Email Marketing – Retention Email Example

## The Context

We noticed that many of our customers are parents with young children.  
With our latest creation — **Zone on the Go** — we decided to launch it with two main focuses: **accessibility** and **time-saving**.  

This email was written specifically for our existing clients who are parents.

## Key Goals & Messaging

- **Make them feel seen and heard**  
  We pay attention to the reality of being a parent with very little free time.

- **Highlight accessibility**  
  The new app lets you join workout classes online and track your progress from **anywhere**.  
  From now on, customers can do their workouts without ever leaving home.

- **Strong incentive**  
  We are offering a **3-month free trial** — plenty of time to try the app, see real benefits, and find out if it truly fits into their busy life.

- **Frictionless next step**  
  Included a clear, easy-to-use **Download** button so they can get the app quickly and start right away.

## Why this email matters

It’s a good example of targeted retention marketing:  
speaking directly to a specific customer segment, acknowledging their lifestyle challenges, and offering meaningful value + a low-risk way to try the product.
